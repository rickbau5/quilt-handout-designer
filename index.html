Properties

Select a shape to edit its properties, or pick a tool on the left to add one.
Tip: drag near the grid or near another shape's edge to snap. Diagrams are just groups of shapes — select the pieces for one diagram and use Export to save just that group. Select a whole group to turn on a black outline around its outside edge, so the block reads against a white page even with piece borders off.
